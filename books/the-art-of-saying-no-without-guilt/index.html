SELF-HELP

The Art of Saying No Without Guilt

How to Set Boundaries, Stop People-Pleasing, and Reclaim Your Time and Energy

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

A practical guide for people-pleasers — the CLEAR Framework for saying no clearly, without guilt or over-explaining.

The Art of Saying No Without Guilt is a self-help guide by Indian author Parvind Kumar for people who say yes when they mean no. It explores why disappointing someone doesn't necessarily mean doing something wrong, and introduces the CLEAR Framework — Consider, Listen, Express, Avoid unnecessary justification, Remain respectful — for turning down requests without excessive explanation.

The book covers boundaries at work, with family, and in relationships, along with ready-to-use 'no' scripts and a 30-Day No-Guilt Challenge that turns the ideas into an everyday habit — without asking readers to reject every request, only to become more intentional about the ones they accept.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Why Saying No Feels So Hard: Guilt, people-pleasing, and the hidden cost of always agreeing.
  • Chapter 2: The CLEAR Framework: Consider, Listen, Express, Avoid justification, Remain respectful.
  • Chapter 3: Boundaries at Work and at Home: Saying no to colleagues, family, and partners.
  • Chapter 4: Handling Pushback: Responding when someone challenges your boundary.
  • Chapter 5: The 30-Day No-Guilt Challenge: Turning boundaries into a lasting habit.
