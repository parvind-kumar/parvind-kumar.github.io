BODY, MIND & SPIRIT

Micchami Dukkadam

The Art of Forgiveness, Letting Go, and Finding Inner Peace

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

Rooted in the Jain tradition of forgiveness, a guide to accountability, letting go of resentment, and self-forgiveness — with a 7-Day Forgiveness Challenge.

Micchami Dukkadam is a book on forgiveness by Indian author Parvind Kumar, drawing its title from the Jain practice of seeking forgiveness from all beings. It explores forgiveness alongside accountability, boundaries and responsibility — not as forgetting or excusing harm, but as acknowledging it, releasing what no longer needs to be carried, and choosing how to move forward.

The book covers sincere apology, the difference between guilt and shame, self-forgiveness, and forgiveness within families, friendships and digital life, closing with a 7-Day Forgiveness Challenge and a 30-Day Journey of guided reflection.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: What Micchami Dukkadam Means: The Jain roots of the practice, and why it still matters.
  • Chapter 2: Why We Hold On: Anger, resentment, and the emotional baggage we carry.
  • Chapter 3: Apology and Accountability: How to apologize sincerely and take responsibility.
  • Chapter 4: Forgiving Without Excusing: Boundaries, justice, and forgiveness that doesn't mean forgetting.
  • Chapter 5: Self-Forgiveness: The difference between guilt and shame, and letting go of self-blame.
  • Chapter 6: The 7-Day Forgiveness Challenge: A guided path toward release and inner peace.
