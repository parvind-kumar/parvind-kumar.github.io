MINDFULNESS

The Complete Guide to Mindfulness & Meditation

A Practical Companion for Daily Presence, Focus, and Everyday Calm

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

A practical journey into mindfulness, meditation, attention and everyday calm.

The Complete Guide to Mindfulness & Meditation is a self-help and wellness book by Indian author Parvind Kumar. In our fast-paced, screen-dominated world, finding quiet is harder than ever. This book demystifies meditation, making it accessible to beginners and regular practitioners alike. It outlines simple breathing exercises, body scans, and mental models to manage stress, reduce anxiety, and reclaim your focus. Beyond formal meditation, the book teaches how to bring mindfulness into everyday actions, from eating and walking to working and communicating.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Understanding Mindfulness: Demystifying what mindfulness is and isn't. Exploring the scientific benefits on brain structure and stress response.
  • Chapter 2: The Breath as an Anchor: Basic breathing techniques (box breathing, diaphragmatic breathing) to calm the nervous system instantly.
  • Chapter 3: Silent Sitting: Steps to Meditate: A step-by-step physical and mental layout for setting up a daily 10-minute meditation habit.
  • Chapter 4: Overcoming the Restless Mind: How to handle thoughts, distractions, and physical discomfort during meditation without judgment.
  • Chapter 5: Mindfulness in Daily Actions: Methods to cultivate awareness during routine chores, eating, walking, and digital interactions.
