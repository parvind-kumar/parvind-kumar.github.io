CHILDREN'S EDUCATION

Learn Abacus

A Step-by-Step Guide to Mental Math, Fast Calculation, and Number Confidence

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

A beginner-friendly abacus guide — from reading beads to mental math, with a 30-Day Abacus Challenge and practice games.

Learn Abacus is a beginner-friendly guide by Indian author Parvind Kumar that takes learners from their first introduction to the abacus to confident mental calculation, following a progressive journey: understand, practice, visualize, calculate and improve.

It covers the parts of the abacus, reading numbers with beads, place value, addition and subtraction, complements and carrying, larger numbers, multiplication and division, and visualization techniques for mental abacus. Puzzles, games, timed challenges, a 30-Day Abacus Challenge, progress tracking and a final mastery test make it useful for students, parents and teachers.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Meet the Abacus: Parts of the abacus and how it works.
  • Chapter 2: Numbers and Place Value: Reading and representing numbers with beads.
  • Chapter 3: Addition and Subtraction: Step by step, with complements and carrying.
  • Chapter 4: Multiplication and Division: Working with larger numbers.
  • Chapter 5: Mental Abacus: Visualization techniques and building speed with accuracy.
  • Chapter 6: The 30-Day Abacus Challenge: Practice, games, progress tracking and a final mastery test.
