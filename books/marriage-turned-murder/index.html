TRUE CRIME

Marriage Turned Murder

India's Most Shocking Husband and Wife Crimes, 20 True Stories

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

Twenty real Indian marriages that ended in murder — built from police statements, court filings, and verified news reports.

Marriage Turned Murder is a true crime collection by Indian author Parvind Kumar documenting twenty real cases where an Indian marriage ended in murder — among them the Blue Drum Murder, the Cobra Plot, the Tandoor Murder, and the Faridkot NRI case, drawn from Delhi, Bihar, Kerala, Punjab, Karnataka, Maharashtra and beyond.

Built from police statements, court filings, and verified news reports, each chapter follows the same structure — the victim, the marriage, the crime, the investigation, and where the case stands today — asking how well anyone truly knows the person sleeping beside them.

Table of Contents

  • Case 1: The Blue Drum Murder: A case that shocked Delhi and exposed a marriage's hidden violence.
  • Case 2: The Cobra Plot: A meticulously planned crime that nearly went undetected.
  • Case 3: The Tandoor Murder: One of India's most infamous marital crimes, revisited.
  • Case 4: The Faridkot NRI Case: A cross-border marriage that ended in tragedy.
  • Cases 5–20: Sixteen More Cases: Sixteen further verified cases from across India, and what they reveal.
