HISTORICAL FICTION

The Weaver's Rebellion

A Textile Town Rises Against Exploitative Colonial Trade Policies, Led by an Unlikely Heroine

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

In a town where every home has a loom, a young weaver leads her community against exploitative colonial trade.

The Weaver's Rebellion is a historical novel by Indian author Parvind Kumar set in the textile town of Sutanagar, where every home has a loom. When colonial trading interests flood the market with machine-made cloth and force weavers to accept unfair prices, debts mount and families begin selling the looms that sustained them.

Madhavi, a young weaver who never imagined herself a leader, begins by fighting to save her father's loom — and ends up bringing the town together with fellow weaver Karim, organizer Amina and others, from small acts of solidarity to a coordinated refusal of exploitative terms. As arrests and pressure follow, she must ask how a rebellion can remain a fight for justice without becoming a quest for revenge.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: A Town of Looms: Life in Sutanagar before the colonial trade policies take hold.
  • Chapter 2: The Machine-Made Flood: Imported textiles, unfair prices and mounting debts.
  • Chapter 3: Madhavi's Choice: A personal fight to save a loom becomes something larger.
  • Chapter 4: Solidarity: Karim, Amina and the community begin to organize.
  • Chapter 5: The Authorities Take Notice: Arrests, pressure on merchants and a town divided.
  • Chapter 6: Justice, Not Revenge: What the rebellion must become to stay a fight for justice.
