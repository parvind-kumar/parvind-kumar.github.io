Life Coaching Guide

How to Be a Life Coach & Launch a Life Coaching Business in Less Than 30 Days

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

A step-by-step guide to becoming a life coach and launching a coaching business in 30 days, with a full toolkit of scripts, worksheets and templates.

Life Coaching Guide is a business playbook by Indian author Parvind Kumar for beginners who want to understand coaching, develop practical skills, and turn them into a professional practice. It covers the C.L.E.A.R. Coaching Framework, finding your niche, defining your ideal client, and designing coaching packages and pricing.

Its centerpiece is a 30-day launch roadmap moving from purpose and niche through discovery calls, onboarding, and your first client — backed by a toolkit of worksheets, scripts, and templates for everything from intake forms to social-media bios.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: What Coaching Really Is: The mindset and skills of an effective coach, and common mistakes to avoid.
  • Chapter 2: The C.L.E.A.R. Coaching Framework: Structuring productive coaching conversations.
  • Chapter 3: Finding Your Niche and Ideal Client: Turning your strengths and experience into a coaching offer.
  • Chapter 4: Packages, Pricing and Positioning: Designing your offer and your professional presence.
  • Chapter 5: The 30-Day Launch Plan: From idea to your first paying client.
