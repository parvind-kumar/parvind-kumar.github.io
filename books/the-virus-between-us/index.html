ROMANCE · THRILLER

The Virus Between Us

One Diagnosis. One Dead Doctor. A Secret That Could Destroy Everything.

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

An HIV diagnosis, a doctor's suspicious death, and a woman who won't stop asking questions — a suspenseful story about stigma, secrets, and redemption.

The Virus Between Us is a romantic thriller by Indian author Parvind Kumar. Maya Sharma's life is upended by a single test result — and further upended when the doctor who delivered it is found dead, in what is officially ruled a suicide. Maya doesn't believe it, and when her own medical records vanish, she realizes someone doesn't want her asking questions.

Her investigation pulls in a former lover, a fiancé with his own secrets, a powerful hospital owner, and a string of patients whose stories were quietly buried. Every answer she finds only opens another question, and every person she trusts turns out to be hiding something.

Beneath its mystery, the novel is also a story about stigma — about what an HIV diagnosis does and doesn't mean, and about a woman deciding to understand her own truth before she can help anyone else understand theirs.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: The Diagnosis: Maya receives the result that changes everything, hours before her doctor is found dead.
  • Chapter 2: Missing Records: A quiet disappearance from the hospital's files convinces Maya the death was no suicide.
  • Chapter 3: Old Ties, New Doubts: A former lover and a hospital owner both know more than they're saying.
  • Chapter 4: What the Patients Knew: Buried case files connect the doctor to a pattern no one was meant to notice.
  • Chapter 5: The Truth About the Virus: Maya confronts what her diagnosis means — and doesn't mean — for her future.
  • Chapter 6: Reckoning: The people hiding the truth are finally forced to answer for it.
