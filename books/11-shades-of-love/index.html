FICTION & RELATIONSHIPS

11 Shades of Love: Modern Indian Stories

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 5 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

A collection of eleven compelling short stories exploring the spectrum of romance in contemporary India. From digital-age encounters to long-distance struggles and second chances, this book captures the nuances of love, heartbreak, and resilience in a rapidly changing society.

11 Shades of Love: Modern Indian Stories is a compelling addition to Parvind Kumar's list of publications, showcasing his deep understanding of contemporary subjects.

Table of Contents

  • Story 1: A dating app's algorithm keeps matching two exes — is it fate, or just good retention design? (Swipe Right, Fall Wrong)
  • Story 2: A caste divide tests a young doctor's engagement to the man who's loved her since college. (The Reservation)
  • Story 3: A private proposal goes viral — and a couple must fight to keep their own story their own. (Trending in 24 Hours)
  • Story 4: A delivery rider and a doctor, reunited ten years after poverty pulled them apart. (Ten Years, One Delivery)
  • Story 5: Two strangers, stranded together by a city-wide protest, meet on the same metro line — again and again. (Metro Line 3)
  • Story 6: On the ghats of Varanasi, a priest's daughter falls for the boy whose family keeps the sacred fire. (Ganga Ki Godh Mein)
  • Story 7: Ten goats, a half-acre plot, and a poor farmer's quiet plan to earn a landlord's respect. (Twenty Goats and a Good Name)
  • Story 8: A fuel protest, a mechanic, and the woman from the transport department who starts listening. (Mileage)
  • Story 9: Two rival campaign managers, one election, and an attraction neither of them can afford. (Election Se Pehle)
  • Story 10: A flood, a rescue, and a week that neither of them can stop looking for again. (What the Water Left Behind)
  • Story 11: A four-month romance built on messages — until one confession changes what "real" even means. (The Chatbot Confession)
