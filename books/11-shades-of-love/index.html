11 Shades of Love: Modern Indian Stories

Stories of Passion, Conflict, Heartbreak, and New Beginnings

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 5 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

Modern Indian love stories of passion, conflict, heartbreak and new beginnings.

11 Shades of Love: Modern Indian Stories is a fiction anthology by Indian author Parvind Kumar. This book gathers eleven moving short stories that capture the sheer diversity of love in contemporary India. From high-tech romance in Bangalore to quiet, shared moments in old Delhi, the stories deal with long-distance struggles, inter-caste marriages, dating apps, and the bittersweet task of moving on. Each story offers a unique window into the human heart, depicting love not as a single fairy tale, but as a rich, complicated spectrum of human emotion.

Table of Contents

  • Story 1: Swipe Right, Fall Wrong: A dating app's algorithm keeps matching two exes — is it fate, or just good retention design?
  • Story 2: The Reservation: A caste divide tests a young doctor's engagement to the man who's loved her since college.
  • Story 3: Trending in 24 Hours: A private proposal goes viral — and a couple must fight to keep their own story their own.
  • Story 4: Ten Years, One Delivery: A delivery rider and a doctor, reunited ten years after poverty pulled them apart.
  • Story 5: Metro Line 3: Two strangers, stranded together by a city-wide protest, meet on the same metro line — again and again.
  • Story 6: Ganga Ki Godh Mein: On the ghats of Varanasi, a priest's daughter falls for the boy whose family keeps the sacred fire.
  • Story 7: Twenty Goats and a Good Name: Ten goats, a half-acre plot, and a poor farmer's quiet plan to earn a landlord's respect.
  • Story 8: Mileage: A fuel protest, a mechanic, and the woman from the transport department who starts listening.
  • Story 9: Election Se Pehle: Two rival campaign managers, one election, and an attraction neither of them can afford.
  • Story 10: What the Water Left Behind: A flood, a rescue, and a week that neither of them can stop looking for again.
  • Story 11: The Chatbot Confession: A four-month romance built on messages — until one confession changes what 'real' even means.
