HEALTH & WELLNESS

The Sex & Body Book

A Judgment-Free Guide to Understanding Your Body, Relationships, Consent, Health & Sexuality

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

A clear, judgment-free guide to bodies, consent, relationships and sexual health — real questions, clear science, no shame.

The Sex & Body Book is a health and education guide by Indian author Parvind Kumar offering an approachable, judgment-free introduction to subjects everyone deserves to understand. It starts from the point that embarrassment, stigma, cultural taboos and misinformation make it hard to find trustworthy answers about sexual health.

The book covers human anatomy, puberty, reproductive health, body diversity and the changes that occur through life; menstruation, contraception, sexually transmitted infections, prevention and testing; consent and boundaries, including how to recognize pressure and manipulation; and building healthier relationships through trust, communication and mutual respect.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Understanding Your Body: Anatomy, puberty, body diversity and change through life.
  • Chapter 2: Sexual and Reproductive Health: Menstruation, contraception, STIs, prevention and testing.
  • Chapter 3: Consent and Boundaries: What consent means and how to recognize pressure or manipulation.
  • Chapter 4: Healthier Relationships: Trust, communication, attraction and mutual respect.
  • Chapter 5: Myths and Misinformation: Separating reliable information from social-media claims.
  • Chapter 6: Getting Reliable Help: When and how to seek trustworthy medical guidance.
