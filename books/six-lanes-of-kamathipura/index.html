★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

Six Lanes of Kamathipura is a gritty, realistic novel exploring the intersection of lives in Mumbai's historic district. It delves into the dreams, struggles, and complex choices of characters trying to find hope in a world shaped by harsh realities. Through rich character development, the book paints an authentic portrait of human resilience.

Six Lanes of Kamathipura is a compelling addition to Parvind Kumar's list of publications, showcasing his deep understanding of contemporary subjects.
