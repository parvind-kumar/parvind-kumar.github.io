The Road to Ladakh

A Journey from Greater Noida West to the Himalayas and Back — Roads, Mountains, Memories, and Lessons from the Journey

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

A family road trip memoir from Greater Noida West to Leh-Ladakh via Manali — roads, mountains, memories and lessons from the journey.

The Road to Ladakh is a travel memoir by Indian author Parvind Kumar following a family road trip from Greater Noida West to Leh-Ladakh via Manali — from the familiar plains of North India into the landscapes of the Himalayas and back home again.

The road is part of the adventure: changing scenery, mountain highways, roadside stops and moments shared in the car. The book reflects on the anticipation before departure, the conversations on the road, the value of preparation and patience, and the feeling of returning home after days somewhere different.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Beginning the Adventure: Planning and packing for the road from Greater Noida West.
  • Chapter 2: The Road to Manali: From the plains into the mountains.
  • Chapter 3: Into the Himalayas: Mountain highways, landscapes and roadside stops.
  • Chapter 4: Leh-Ladakh: Arriving in a place unlike anywhere else.
  • Chapter 5: The Journey Back: Memories and the return home.
  • Chapter 6: Lessons from the Road: Travel, family, preparation and patience.
