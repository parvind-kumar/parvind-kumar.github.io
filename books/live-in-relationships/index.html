Live-In Relationships: The Untold Truth

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

This book provides a comprehensive, non-judgmental look at the dynamics of modern cohabitation in India. From emotional expectations and societal pressure to financial management and legal aspects, the author provides valuable insights for couples considering taking this step, backed by real-life stories and practical advice.

Live-In Relationships: The Untold Truth is a compelling addition to Parvind Kumar's list of publications, showcasing his deep understanding of contemporary subjects.
