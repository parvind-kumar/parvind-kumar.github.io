MYTHOLOGY

Khatu Shyam

The Untold Story of Barbarika, the Divine Sacrifice, and the Lord Who Became the Refuge of the Defeated

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

The story of Barbarika, his three arrows and Sheesh Daan, and how he came to be worshipped as Khatu Shyam.

Khatu Shyam is a mythology and devotion title by Indian author Parvind Kumar telling the story of Barbarika — the Mahabharata warrior of the three powerful arrows who was committed to keeping his word — and how he came to be worshipped as Khatu Shyam in the Kali Yuga.

The book follows his lineage and abilities, the symbolism of the Three Arrows, his promise to support the weaker side, Krishna's test at Kurukshetra and the meaning of Sheesh Daan. It also explains names such as Hare Ka Sahara, Sheesh Ke Daani and Teen Baan Dhari, and the devotional traditions of Khatu Dham, Shyam Kund and the Nishan Yatra.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Barbarika the Three-Arrow Warrior: His lineage, childhood and legendary abilities.
  • Chapter 2: The Mystery of the Three Arrows: Their symbolism and his promise to support the weaker side.
  • Chapter 3: Krishna's Test at Kurukshetra: The encounter that changed Barbarika's destiny.
  • Chapter 4: Sheesh Daan: The meaning of his sacrifice and Krishna's divine promise.
  • Chapter 5: Becoming Khatu Shyam: Names such as Hare Ka Sahara, Sheesh Ke Daani and Teen Baan Dhari.
  • Chapter 6: Khatu Dham and Devotion: The temple, Shyam Kund, Nishan Yatra and major traditions.
