CONTEMPORARY ROMANCE

The American Distance

Two People. One Country Divided. A Love That Refuses to Choose Sides.

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

An immigration lawyer and a tech executive fall for each other just as she uncovers that the algorithm hurting her client was built by his own company.

The American Distance is a contemporary romance novel by Indian author Parvind Kumar. Maya Rao, an immigration lawyer fighting to keep her client Sofia from being deported over an unexplained fraud flag, collides — almost literally, over a spilled coffee — with Ethan Carter, a rising executive at the AI company behind the very software making that decision.

What begins as an accidental meeting becomes something neither of them planned for, even as Maya digs deeper into the system quietly mapping and selling the American public, and discovers the man she can't stop thinking about helped build it.

The novel follows Ethan's reckoning with what his company is really building, and Maya's own lesson that being right isn't always the same as being careful — a love story that refuses to look away from the country, and the systems, it's set inside.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: A Spilled Coffee: Maya and Ethan meet outside a federal building, on opposite sides of a fight neither has named yet.
  • Chapter 2: Four Minutes to Be Somewhere: Taco-cart dates and a dinner where Ethan lies about a company name without quite meaning to.
  • Chapter 3: What the Algorithm Decided: Maya discovers the system flagging her client as fraud — and who built it.
  • Chapter 4: The Distance Between Them: Ethan is pulled deeper into what Halcyon AI is really building.
  • Chapter 5: Putting a Name on It: Ethan decides what it means to finally stand behind something hard.
