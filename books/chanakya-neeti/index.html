Ancient Wisdom for Modern Life, Leadership, Money, Power, and Success

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

Principles traditionally associated with Chanakya, applied to modern life, leadership, money, relationships and the digital age.

Chanakya Neeti is a self-help and strategy book by Indian author Parvind Kumar exploring the principles traditionally associated with Chanakya — also known as Kautilya or Vishnugupta — and interpreting them for contemporary life. It notes the difference between the Chanakya Niti tradition, concerned with human behaviour and everyday life, and the Arthashastra, a distinct and far more extensive work on governance and statecraft.

The book covers self-discipline, controlling anger and ego, choosing friends, protecting reputation, managing money, career and leadership, negotiation, handling difficult people, crisis management, family and relationships, entrepreneurship, and even privacy and artificial intelligence — asking how ancient strategic wisdom can help us think more clearly and act more wisely.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Who Was Chanakya?: Chanakya, Kautilya and Vishnugupta, and the difference between Niti and the Arthashastra.
  • Chapter 2: Self-Discipline and the Inner Enemies: Controlling anger, fear, greed and ego.
  • Chapter 3: People, Friends and Reputation: Understanding human behaviour and choosing trustworthy relationships.
  • Chapter 4: Money, Career and Leadership: Earning, saving, decision-making and professional success.
  • Chapter 5: Strategy, Competition and Crisis: Negotiation, preparation, risk and dealing with difficult people.
  • Chapter 6: Chanakya in the Digital Age: Privacy, technological change and artificial intelligence.
