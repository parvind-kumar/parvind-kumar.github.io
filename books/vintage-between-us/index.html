A Tuscan vineyard, two people and a romance caught between past and present.

The Vintage Between Us: A Tuscan Vineyard Romance is a romantic drama by Indian author Parvind Kumar. Set in the rolling hills and sun-drenched vineyards of Tuscany, the novel tells the story of an Indian architect, Kabir, who travels to Italy to restore an old family estate, and Alessia, a local winemaker determined to save her family's heritage. As they collaborate to rebuild the ancient property, they uncover a decades-old secret connecting their families, forcing them to choose between honoring their family duties and following their hearts. It is a slow-burn romance rich in atmosphere, wine culture, and emotional depth.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Autumn in Tuscany: Kabir arrives in Italy, met with the breathtaking beauty of the vineyard and the cold skepticism of Alessia.
  • Chapter 2: The Legacy of the Vine: Learning the art of winemaking and uncovering the historical significance of the estate.
  • Chapter 3: Shared Sunsets: Conversations over local dinners build a bridge of understanding between Kabir and Alessia.
  • Chapter 4: Fermenting Secrets: Letters found in an old cellar chest reveal a forbidden romance from the 1960s.
  • Chapter 5: Harvest and Heartbreak: The peak of the harvest season brings emotional conflicts and family ultimatums to a boil.
  • Chapter 6: The Vintage Uncorked: Resolving past family guilt and building a shared future among the vines.
