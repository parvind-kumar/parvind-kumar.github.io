The Vintage Between Us: A Tuscan Vineyard Romance

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

Set against the backdrop of rolling hills and warm sun-drenched vineyards of Tuscany, The Vintage Between Us is a sweeping romantic novel. When two people from vastly different worlds meet at an old vineyard, they find themselves caught between their complicated pasts and a passionate present. A rich, slow-burn romance about healing and starting anew.

The Vintage Between Us: A Tuscan Vineyard Romance is a compelling addition to Parvind Kumar's list of publications, showcasing his deep understanding of contemporary subjects.
