SUSPENSE

Crime Suspense Stories

Anthology of Mystery, Secrets, and Dark Human Choices

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

Stories built around mystery, crime, secrets and the consequences of hidden choices.

Crime Suspense Stories is a gripping fiction collection by Indian author Parvind Kumar. Perfect for fans of mysteries and psychological dramas, the book compiles several thrilling stories where ordinary people find themselves in extraordinary, dangerous situations. The narratives explore themes of betrayal, greed, the flawless alibi, and the ultimate psychological toll of crime. With sharp pacing, sharp dialogues, and unexpected plot twists, this anthology keeps readers on the edge of their seats until the final revelation.

Table of Contents

  • Story 1: The Locked Study: A late-night mystery in a locked study room leads to a shocking family discovery.
  • Story 2: Footprints in the Dust: Unusual tracks at a crime scene become the key clue for a determined investigator.
  • Story 3: A False Alibi: A suspect creates an elaborate alibi, only to be betrayed by a small digital footprint.
  • Story 4: The Witness's Silence: A witness refuses to speak out, hiding a dark secret that could alter the entire case.
  • Story 5: Red Herring: A series of misleading clues leads the police away from the real mastermind.
  • Story 6: Final Judgment: A high-stakes resolution where justice is served in the most unexpected way.
