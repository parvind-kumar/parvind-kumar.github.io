101 Public Speaking Tips

Master the Stage, Own the Room, Change Minds

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

101 actionable techniques for public speaking — from conquering fear to structuring speeches, vocal delivery, and a 30-day action plan.

101 Public Speaking Tips is a practical guide by Indian author Parvind Kumar covering the complete public-speaking journey in 101 actionable techniques — conquering fear without waiting to feel ready, researching an audience, structuring stronger openings and conclusions, and mastering vocal delivery, pace, and pauses.

The book also covers body language and stage presence, storytelling, audience interaction, presentation design, and handling difficult Q&A. It closes with a 30-Day Public Speaking Action Plan that pushes readers to practice progressively, record themselves, and ultimately deliver a speech to a live audience.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Conquering Fear: Building confidence without waiting to feel ready.
  • Chapter 2: Structure and Preparation: Stronger openings, transitions, and conclusions.
  • Chapter 3: Vocal Delivery and Body Language: Pace, pitch, pauses, and stage presence.
  • Chapter 4: Storytelling and Engagement: Making ideas memorable and keeping an audience involved.
  • Chapter 5: Slides, Q&A and Difficult Moments: Presenting with clarity and handling pressure with composure.
  • Chapter 6: The 30-Day Action Plan: A structured path from nervous speaker to confident one.
