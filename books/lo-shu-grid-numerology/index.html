NUMEROLOGY

The Complete Guide to Lo Shu Grid Numerology

Discover the Ancient Chinese Magic Square and Remedies for Balance and Prosperity

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

An accessible guide to understanding the Lo Shu Grid and its traditional numerological framework.

The Complete Guide to Lo Shu Grid Numerology is a practical non-fiction book by Indian author Parvind Kumar. It introduces readers to the ancient Chinese system of numerology based on the Lo Shu Grid—a magic square of order 3. The book provides clear, step-by-step instructions on how to construct a grid using birth dates, analyze the strength of different numbers, understand the impact of missing numbers, and determine auspicious directions. Written in an accessible and easy-to-understand format, it offers practical remedies such as colors, elements, and gemstones to bring balance, health, and prosperity into your life.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Origin of Lo Shu Grid: The history and mythology behind the ancient magic square discovered on a tortoise shell.
  • Chapter 2: The Nine Numbers and Meanings: Detailed characteristics, elemental associations, and significance of numbers 1 through 9.
  • Chapter 3: Constructing Your Grid: A practical guide to placing birth dates into the grid and calculating basic driving numbers.
  • Chapter 4: Analyzing Strengths and Weaknesses: Understanding vertical, horizontal, and diagonal planes (willpower, action, intellect, etc.).
  • Chapter 5: Missing Numbers and Remedies: How to balance the energy of missing grid coordinates using elements, crystals, and simple home remedies.
