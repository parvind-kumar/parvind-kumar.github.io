★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

Escape the Endless Scroll is an essential guide to reclaiming your time and focus in the digital age. It explores the psychological mechanics behind digital distractions and social media algorithms, offering realistic and effective techniques to build healthy digital boundaries, curb mindless screen-time, and rediscover screen-free joy.

Escape the Endless Scroll is a compelling addition to Parvind Kumar's list of publications, showcasing his deep understanding of contemporary subjects.
