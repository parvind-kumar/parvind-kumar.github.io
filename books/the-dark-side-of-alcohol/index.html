HEALTH & WELLNESS

The Dark Side of Alcohol

The Hidden Health Risks, Addiction, and Devastating Consequences of Drinking

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

The science behind alcohol's effects on the brain, liver, heart and sleep, its links to cancer, and how drinking can become dependence.

The Dark Side of Alcohol is a health book by Indian author Parvind Kumar examining the science, social beliefs and personal consequences of one of the world's most widely consumed psychoactive substances. It looks at how alcohol is processed and how drinking can affect the brain, liver, heart, digestive system, sleep and overall health.

The book covers the evidence linking alcohol with several types of cancer, the psychology of drinking — habits, cravings, tolerance and dependence — common myths about beer, wine, relaxation and moderation, and the hidden cost of drinking on relationships, parenting and wider life.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Alcohol in Everyday Culture: Why drinking is so widely accepted.
  • Chapter 2: What Alcohol Does to the Body: The brain, liver, heart, digestion and sleep.
  • Chapter 3: Alcohol and Cancer Risk: What the scientific evidence says.
  • Chapter 4: The Psychology of Drinking: Habits, cravings, tolerance and dependence.
  • Chapter 5: Myths and Misconceptions: Beer, wine, relaxation, sleep and the meaning of moderation.
  • Chapter 6: The Hidden Cost: Effects on relationships, parenting and daily life.
