SPIRITUALITY

The Truth About Osho

His Teachings on Meditation, Love, Freedom, Sex, and the Search for Enlightenment

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

Osho's philosophy of meditation, love and freedom alongside the controversies of his movement — a critical, independent look.

The Truth About Osho is a critical study by Indian author Parvind Kumar of Osho, formerly Bhagwan Shree Rajneesh, one of the most debated spiritual figures of modern times. It examines both his provocative teachings — on meditation, awareness, love, personal freedom and the search for meaning — and the controversies surrounding his movement.

The book covers his challenges to organized religion and social conditioning, witnessing and Dynamic Meditation, his views on love, sex, jealousy and attachment, the idea of Zorba the Buddha, the rise of the international movement and Rajneeshpuram in Oregon, and the criminal proceedings and serious questions raised about spiritual authority, wealth and loyalty. It invites readers to avoid both blind belief and blind rejection.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Who Was Osho?: From Rajneesh to Osho.
  • Chapter 2: The Philosophy: Challenges to organized religion and conditioning.
  • Chapter 3: Meditation and Awareness: Witnessing and Dynamic Meditation.
  • Chapter 4: Love, Sex and Freedom: Intimacy, desire, jealousy and attachment.
  • Chapter 5: The Movement and Rajneeshpuram: The rise of the international following and the controversies.
  • Chapter 6: Thinking Independently: Which teachings endure and which claims deserve scrutiny.
