How to Start a Business Without Any Money

Complete Guide Filled with Business Ideas, Business Plans, Tips & Tricks to Make Money Easily Online

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

A practical guide to starting a business with little or no capital — 50+ ideas, one-page business plans, and ways to find your first customer.

How to Start a Business Without Any Money is a practical entrepreneurship guide by Indian author Parvind Kumar for people with limited or no starting capital. It argues that skills, knowledge, time, relationships and technology can be the foundation of a business, and focuses on what readers can actually do: identify opportunities, test ideas before spending money, find a first customer, make a first sale and build something sustainable.

Inside are 50+ businesses that can be started with little or no money, a simple one-page business plan with 10 ready-to-use examples, pricing guidance, ways to get customers without expensive advertising, selling without feeling like a salesperson, and building an online business and personal brand with free tools.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Start With What You Already Have: Turning skills, knowledge and time into income.
  • Chapter 2: Finding and Choosing an Idea: Spotting real problems and picking the right idea for your situation.
  • Chapter 3: Testing Before You Spend: Validating an idea and finding your first customer.
  • Chapter 4: Offers, Pricing and the One-Page Plan: Creating an offer people understand, with 10 sample business plans.
  • Chapter 5: 50+ Low-Cost Business Ideas: Businesses you can start with little or no money.
  • Chapter 6: Growing Online on a Budget: Free tools, social media and a personal brand without a large budget.
