SELF-HELP

Mind Over Noise

Practical Tools to Filter Distraction and Think Clearly in an Overstimulated World

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

Practical frameworks for filtering notifications, information overload and mental clutter — building a clearer mind without disconnecting from modern life.

Mind Over Noise is a self-help guide by Indian author Parvind Kumar built on a simple premise: you can't control everything competing for your attention, but you can get better at deciding what deserves it. Rather than asking readers to abandon their phones, it offers frameworks — the NOISE framework, an Attention Budget, a Distraction Ladder, an Opinion Filter — for using modern life more deliberately.

The book covers clearing a mental inbox, separating information from knowledge, protecting the first hour of the day, and building a Personal Clarity System, closing with a 30-Day Mind Over Noise Challenge that moves from seeing the noise to living beyond it.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: The Many Forms of Noise: Notifications, information overload, and constant switching.
  • Chapter 2: Clearing the Mental Desk: Separating information from knowledge, and organizing unfinished thoughts.
  • Chapter 3: The Attention Budget: Deciding deliberately what deserves your focus.
  • Chapter 4: Boundaries and the Digital Sabbath: Protecting the first hour of the day and building intentional disconnection.
  • Chapter 5: The 30-Day Mind Over Noise Challenge: From seeing the noise to living beyond it.
