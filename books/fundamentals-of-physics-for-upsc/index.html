UPSC PREPARATION

Fundamentals of Physics for UPSC

A Complete Beginner's Guide to Mechanics, Heat, Light, Sound, Electricity, Magnetism and Modern Physics

by Parvind Kumar

★★★★★ 4.8 / 5 (Goodreads Ratings)

A beginner-friendly physics foundation for UPSC and competitive exams — mechanics, heat, waves, light, electricity and modern physics.

Fundamentals of Physics for UPSC is a beginner's guide by Indian author Parvind Kumar designed to help Civil Services aspirants and other competitive-exam candidates build a strong foundation in basic physics. It introduces concepts step by step in accessible language, even for readers who have not studied physics in depth.

The book covers physical quantities and units, mechanics (motion, Newton's laws, gravity, work, power and energy), heat and thermodynamics, waves and sound, light and optics, electricity and magnetism, atomic, nuclear and modern physics, and physics in everyday life and technology. It emphasizes concept-focused learning, real-world applications and concise revision summaries.

Table of Contents

  • Chapter 1: Fundamentals of Physics: Physical quantities, units, measurement, matter, density and pressure.
  • Chapter 2: Mechanics: Motion, force, Newton's laws, gravity, work, power and energy.
  • Chapter 3: Heat and Thermodynamics: Temperature, heat transfer and the basic laws of thermodynamics.
  • Chapter 4: Waves and Sound: Frequency, amplitude, echoes and ultrasound.
  • Chapter 5: Light, Electricity and Magnetism: Optics, circuits, electromagnetism, motors and transformers.
  • Chapter 6: Modern Physics and Technology: Atomic and nuclear physics, lasers, semiconductors and space.
