ABOUT PARVIND KUMAR

Writing about the questions we don't always ask out loud.

Parvind Kumar is an independent author whose work moves across fiction, relationships, marriage, personal growth and contemporary social themes. His books are written for readers who enjoy accessible ideas, emotionally engaging stories and questions that continue after the last page.

From modern Indian relationships to mindfulness, suspense and everyday human choices, his catalogue reflects a simple belief: a good book should make you feel something — and think about it later.

Parvind Kumar
